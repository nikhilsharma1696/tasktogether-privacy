TaskTogether - Account Deletion

TaskTogether is a productivity application that helps individuals and groups organize tasks and manage shared responsibilities.

Users can permanently delete their TaskTogether account through the application or request account deletion by contacting our support team.

1. Delete your account through the app

  1. Open the TaskTogether application.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Navigate to Settings > Account.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Follow the confirmation instructions.
Account deletion is permanent. Please ensure that you no longer need your personal account information before confirming deletion.

2. Request account deletion by email

If you cannot access your account or encounter difficulties deleting it through the application, you can request deletion by email.

Send your request to: tasktogether.support@gmail.com

Email subject: TaskTogether Account Deletion Request

Please send your request from the email address associated with your TaskTogether account whenever possible. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

3. Data deleted with your account

When your TaskTogether account is deleted, the following account-associated data is deleted:

  • Account profile and personal information
  • Email address and authentication records
  • Personal tasks and related task information
  • Group memberships associated with the account
  • Notification preferences and device tokens

4. Shared tasks and group information

TaskTogether supports collaboration through private groups.

Shared tasks and group information belonging to other members may remain available to those members after your account is deleted.

Your deleted account and its associated personal information are not retained as part of those shared records.

5. Data retention

TaskTogether does not retain deleted account data following account deletion. Account deletion is permanent, and deleted account data cannot be recovered.

6. Contact us

For account deletion requests or questions about personal data, contact:

tasktogether.support@gmail.com
