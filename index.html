TaskTogether Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you use TaskTogether.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

TaskTogether is a task-management and collaboration application (referred to in this policy as “TaskTogether,” “we,” or “us”). This policy describes the personal information processed when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and authentication information: email address, account display information, account identifiers, and information needed to sign you in. For email/password accounts, we store a password hash rather than your plaintext password. If you use Google sign-in, we process the account information needed to authenticate you.
  • Tasks and collaboration content: task titles and descriptions, dates and times, completion status, assignments, group names, membership and invitations, activity history, and messages or acknowledgements you choose to create.
  • Notification information: notification delivery tokens, reminders, notification preferences, and notifications associated with tasks and groups.
  • Operational information: technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the application and its backend, which may be processed by our infrastructure providers.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts; provide personal and shared task management; coordinate group memberships and assignments; deliver reminders and notifications; maintain service reliability and security; respond to support requests; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Collaboration and visibility

When you participate in a group, information you contribute to shared tasks and group activity may be visible to other authorized members of that group. Do not include sensitive personal information in shared task descriptions unless you intend for the relevant group members to see it.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use service providers to operate TaskTogether, including cloud application hosting and database infrastructure, and authentication or push-notification services where applicable. Based on the current implementation, these services may include Render (application hosting), Neon (database hosting), and Google services (Google sign-in and Android push notifications). These providers process information as needed to deliver their services. We also share collaboration content with other authorized group members as described above.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect users and the service, or as part of a legitimate service transfer subject to applicable legal requirements.

5. Data storage and security

Account and task information is processed through TaskTogether's backend and stored using cloud infrastructure. We use authentication and access-control mechanisms intended to limit access to authorized users. No system can guarantee absolute security. Please do not share your password or authentication credentials.

6. Data retention and account deletion

We retain information while it is needed to provide TaskTogether and for legitimate operational or legal purposes. You may request account or personal-data deletion by emailing tasktogether.support@gmail.com from the email associated with your account. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

For eligible password-based accounts, an in-app deletion flow may also be available. Some shared tasks, group records, and activity history may remain for other members after an account is deleted, with account references removed or de-identified where supported. Information in another user's free-text content may not be automatically removed. Backup or legally required records may persist for a limited period where applicable. Contact us if you want to understand what information can be removed in your circumstances.

7. Your choices and rights

You can manage task and group content through available app features and control Android notification permissions in your device settings. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information. Contact us to exercise those rights.

8. Children

TaskTogether is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

9. International processing

Our service providers may process or store information outside your country of residence. Where required, we take steps to address applicable cross-border data-protection obligations.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our app and data practices change. We will publish the updated policy on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes may be communicated through the app or other appropriate means.
